Common questions.

Straight answers, no fine print.

Does Carolina Oil Recovery really pay for used cooking oil?

Yes. We pick your oil up free, and we pay you for it every quarter. How much depends on how much oil you go through and the market price for used cooking oil. Call us and we will give you a straight answer for your kitchen — no guessing.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Your container is free, installation is free, and pickup is free.

Do I have to sign an agreement?

We set every account up on a simple service agreement, so your pickup schedule, your container, and your quarterly payments are in writing. Call us and we will walk you through it before you sign anything.

How fast can you start service?

Fast. Call us, we place your free locked container, and pickups start on a regular route — weekly or every two weeks, based on how much oil you use.

What happens to the oil you collect?

It gets recycled. Used cooking oil is refined into biodiesel and other products. It should never go down a drain — that is what clogs pipes and backs up grease traps.

Do you clean grease traps too?

Yes — that is the point of one vendor for both. We pump and clean indoor traps and outdoor interceptors, and every grease trap visit comes with a signed manifest for your records and inspections.
